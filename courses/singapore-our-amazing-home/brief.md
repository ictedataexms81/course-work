## Learner profile

Learners are five-year-old preschool children. They can listen to a short read-aloud, are developing oral-language and turn-taking skills, and can notice simple shapes and pictures. Materials are read aloud, supported by images and movement, and scribed by an adult when needed. Reading and independent device use are not prerequisites.

The final two practical hours are the supervised project studio and assessment. No separate revision hours are allocated. Evidence is gathered from each child's observable contribution, conversation, and reflection; the group artefact alone is not enough to infer an individual child's learning.

## AI-assist and safeguarding

The planning target is 7 AI-assist hours, the ceiling of 40% of total contact hours. The 40% target is unusually high for five-year-olds and is not evidence that direct AI use is developmentally appropriate. For this pack, an educator controls any approved tool, checks output before use, and presents short printed or otherwise off-screen extracts where feasible. Children make an unaided prediction or drawing first, compare the vetted output, and explain what they notice. Do not collect children's names, images, voices, family details, or other personal data in an AI tool. Do not let children use public chat interfaces. The provider must approve the method under its safeguarding, consent, and data-protection policies before delivery; if approval is not available, the AI target cannot responsibly be met and requires formal revision.

## Assumptions & Gaps

- The supplied document is a child-facing explainer, not a formal syllabus. Six weeks and 17 hours (5 theory, 12 practical) are planning values chosen to support five paired concept-and-activity weeks plus one project week.
- Five competencies are derived from the five themes in the source. Each receives one theory hour and two practical hours; the final project uses two practical hours. There is no separate revision block.
- Provider, module code, framework, duration, workload, delivery mode, class size, marker ratio, prior knowledge, and approved media/tools were not specified. Values above are defaults and must be confirmed locally.
- The 40% AI-assist target and two-hour lab block are orchestrator defaults. Seven hours follows `ceil(17 × 40%)`; the target is exceptionally high for this age group and must not override safeguarding or developmental appropriateness.
- The AI policy is adapted to educator-mediated use. No child should directly prompt a public AI tool or submit personal information. Approval and a suitable tool are unresolved dependencies.
- Criteria are derived from the explainer, not an external standard. Teachers should adapt for language, disability, culture, and individual development.
- Source simplifications require careful treatment: Singapore is an island city-state; Supertrees are built structures with plants; the Rain Vortex is an indoor waterfall at Jewel Changi Airport; not every resident shares the same language, food, or celebrations; and the Merlion is a symbol/statue, not a magical animal.
- Obtain permissions for photographs and stories about children or families. Prefer generic, licensed images and fictional examples; none were supplied with the source.
- The single group project assessment is a planning default at 100%. Provider approval is needed for the evidence method and group assessment format.
